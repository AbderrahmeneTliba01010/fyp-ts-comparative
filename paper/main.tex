\section{Introduction}
Time-series forecasting has moved from task-specific Transformers \citep{zhou2021informer,wu2021autoformer,zhou2022fedformer,nie2023patchtst}, to reusing language models \citep{gruver2023llmtime,zhou2023gpt4ts,jin2024timellm}, to natively pretrained foundation models \citep{ansari2024chronos,das2024timesfm,woo2024moirai,goswami2024moment,liu2025sundial}. Claims across these families are rarely comparable: different datasets, windows, context lengths, metrics, and unknown pretraining overlap. Meanwhile, simple ablations question the value of language-model weights \citep{tan2024language}.
